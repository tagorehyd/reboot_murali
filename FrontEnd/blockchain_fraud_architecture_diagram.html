High-Risk Blockchain Freeze Protocol
When a High-Risk, High-Value txn is initiated and Receiver's Bank is NOT on the same Blockchain Network:

* Receiver's Bank pulls funds into Receiver's account, but only up to a designated time window.
* If Sender confirms fraud within that window, funds are locked and transaction is reversed & blocked in the Unified Ledger.
* Smart Contract Services provisioned to user based on transaction type & initiation method.

Escrow protection until sender fraud signal or action window expires.
